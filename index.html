What we build

Remodeling for the way East Texas homes are built.

Pier-and-beam floors, pine framing, red clay and humid summers call for work done the local way. Every job below comes with a written scope and a fixed price before we start.

Kitchen remodels

Cabinets, counters, islands, lighting and plumbing moved to where you cook. Full tear-outs and refreshes.

  • Custom and semi-custom cabinetry
  • Quartz, granite and butcher block
  • Layout changes with permits pulled

Bathroom remodels

Walk-in showers, tubs, tile, vanities and ventilation that keeps up with East Texas humidity.

  • Curbless and tiled showers
  • Aging-in-place grab bars and benches
  • Moisture-rated drywall and exhaust

Room additions

Bedrooms, in-law suites, home offices and garage conversions, tied into the existing roof line and foundation.

  • Slab and pier-and-beam foundations
  • Matched siding, brick and roofing
  • City of Nacogdoches permits handled

Whole-home renovations

Open a floor plan, replace flooring throughout, update every room at once with one schedule and one point of contact.

  • Load-bearing wall removal with engineering
  • Electrical and plumbing updates
  • Flooring, paint and trim throughout

Porches, decks and outdoor living

Screened porches, covered patios and decks built for mosquito season and summer evenings under the pines.

  • Screened and covered porches
  • Composite and treated-pine decks
  • Outdoor kitchens and fans

Historic home restoration

Careful work on the older homes around Virginia Avenue, Washington Square and Zion Hill, keeping original trim and windows where they can be saved.

  • Pier-and-beam leveling and sill repair
  • Window and millwork restoration
  • Historic-district review assistance
How a project runs

Four steps from first visit to final walk-through.

No surprises on price or schedule. You see the number before demolition starts, and the same crew sees the job through.

Walk-through

Design and fixed quote

Build

Walk-through and warranty
